Every answer is checked before you see it

The model designs. A real simulator decides.

Externally verified

Digital answers are evaluated row by row by Logisim Evolution running the actual emitted file. Analog answers are simulated by LTspice and checked against the question’s own figures, operating regimes included.

Refuses instead of bluffing

Sequential logic, unmeasured parts, analog without LTspice — refused with the reason named. A wrong answer is structurally harder than no answer.

The file is the answer

You get the .circ or .asc the simulator actually checked, drawn the way a person draws it. Open it in the real tool and keep working.

Nothing hidden

Every result states what was checked, by which tool, and what no check can establish. Rejected attempts are shown, not hidden.

How it works

01

Install

The digital evaluator is bundled — decoder and gate questions work out of the box. For analog, install LTspice (free); the app tells you if it’s missing.

02

Add a free model key

The first-run screen links to free signups and stores your key encrypted on your machine. It is never uploaded by Ohmwork.

03

Ask, then check the reading

Every answer starts with how your question was understood. The simulator proves the circuit matches that reading — confirming the reading matches your question is the one check that’s yours.

Built on one rule

The model never grades its own work.

Wrong designs die in private. A circuit the simulator disagrees with is thrown away and redone. If nothing passes, you get an honest failure — never a confident guess.

Chips are measured, not remembered. A question naming a 7447 is checked against a real 7447’s own evaluated behaviour, not against anyone’s memory of a datasheet.

Weaker claims look weaker. Analog results say “measured”, never “verified” — checking a few numbers is a smaller promise than checking every row, and the app refuses to let the two look alike.

Every published number is traceable. The solved library carries the files, the tool versions, and the checks behind each answer — audit it without trusting us.

Your lab question in.
A simulator-checked circuit out.

Type an electronics open-ended question in plain English. Ohmwork designs the circuit, then makes a real simulator prove it — Logisim Evolution for digital, LTspice for analog — and hands you the circuit file plus the answer, with exactly what was checked stated on every result.

Download for Windows free · ~240 MB · digital evaluator included

Windows will warn that the app is unrecognised — it is not code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

Three steps

1Install

Logisim Evolution is bundled inside — digital questions work out of the box. For analog, install LTspice (free); the app tells you if it is missing.

2Add a free model key

The first-run screen links to free signups (Groq, Google AI Studio) and stores your key encrypted on your machine. It is never uploaded by Ohmwork.

3Ask

Decoders, encoders, 7447 displays, regulators, rectifiers. Download the .circ / .asc and open it in the real tool.

What makes it trustworthy

The model never grades its own work. Every digital answer is evaluated row-by-row by Logisim Evolution running the actual emitted file; every analog answer is simulated by LTspice and checked against the question's own figures, operating regimes included. A design the simulator disagrees with is thrown away and redone — nothing reaches you unchecked.

It refuses instead of bluffing. Questions outside its reach — sequential logic, unmeasured parts, analog without LTspice — are refused with the reason named, never answered with a guess.

One check is yours. Every answer starts with THE READING: how your question was understood. The simulator proves the circuit matches the reading; only you can confirm the reading matches your question. That line is printed on every answer because it is true.
